About me

I am a research associate in Coatings and Polymeric Materials at North Dakota State University and a senior cheminformatics and data-science consultant. My work combines molecular descriptors, machine learning, statistics, complex networks, computational chemistry, and scientific programming to create decision-support models for materials and chemical systems.

Across more than 15 years of research, I have worked on polymer property prediction, safer coating design, aquatic toxicity, photophysical materials, nanomedicine, enzyme inhibitors, protein bioinformatics, and perturbation-theory machine learning. I particularly value models that are scientifically interpretable, reproducible, and useful to experimental collaborators.
