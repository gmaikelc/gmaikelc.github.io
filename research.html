Research

Computational methods that connect chemical structure to useful decisions.

My research combines chemical knowledge, molecular representation, machine learning, and simulation to understand materials and biological systems across scales.

Research directions

Connecting molecular representation, simulation, and interpretable AI.

My research develops computational approaches that connect chemical structure and molecular-scale behavior with predictive models across molecular and materials systems. Three complementary directions provide a framework for current work and emerging questions.

01

AI & Cheminformatics

Developing interpretable machine-learning, graph-neural-network, and multitask approaches for molecular and materials property prediction, with emphasis on chemically meaningful representations, uncertainty, and reproducible modeling.

Emerging directions. Integrating graph-based and condition-aware representations to improve transferability across chemical space, compositions, and experimental conditions.

  • Machine learning
  • Graph neural networks
  • Explainable AI
  • Multitask learning
02

Polymers & Functional Materials

Combining polymer informatics and molecular modeling to understand transport, glass-transition behavior, coatings, multicomponent formulations, interfaces, and structure–property relationships in functional materials.

Emerging directions. Linking polymer chemistry, composition, temperature, and molecular-scale interactions to predictive models for transport and thermophysical properties.

  • Polymer informatics
  • Permeability
  • Coatings
  • Materials design
03

Multiscale Molecular Modeling

Using density functional theory, molecular dynamics, adsorption modeling, and data-driven analysis to investigate surfaces, interfaces, molecular interactions, and functional chemical systems across electronic and molecular scales.

Emerging directions. Coupling simulation-derived descriptors with interpretable AI to connect interfacial chemistry and molecular mechanisms with experimentally testable materials behavior.

  • DFT
  • Molecular dynamics
  • Surfaces & interfaces
  • Adsorption
