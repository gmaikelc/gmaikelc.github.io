Research

Computational methods that connect chemical structure to useful decisions.

My research combines chemical knowledge, molecular representation, machine learning, and simulation to understand materials and biological systems across scales.

Current research directions

Polymer property prediction

Developing QSPR and explainable machine-learning models for glass-transition temperature, permeability, dielectric properties, compatibility, photodegradation, and related polymer behavior.

  • QSPR
  • Explainable AI
  • Polymer descriptors

Fouling-release and safer coatings

Modeling structure–property relationships in coating systems and predicting biological and environmental responses to support non-toxic, high-performance materials.

  • Marine biofouling
  • Ecotoxicology
  • Coating design

Complex mixtures and materials

Creating additive and interaction-aware representations for mixtures, composite formulations, deep eutectic solvents, and other multicomponent systems.

  • MixtureMetrics
  • CombinatorixPy
  • Feature engineering

Molecular and bioactivity modeling

Building machine-learning, graph-neural-network, virtual-screening, and multi-target models for molecular properties, bioactivity, ADME, and drug–target interactions.

  • Cheminformatics
  • Graph neural networks
  • Multitask learning
  • Virtual screening

Research philosophy

High-performing models are most valuable when they also respect chemical knowledge, quantify uncertainty, remain reproducible, and lead to experimentally testable decisions.
